📘 Learn the 5 Words

1. Observe — निरीक्षण करना

Example: Observe the stars carefully.

2. Discover — खोज करना / पता लगाना

Example: Scientists discover new things.

3. Explore — खोजबीन करना

Example: We explore the world around us.

4. Experiment — प्रयोग

Example: We perform an experiment in the lab.

5. Conclusion — निष्कर्ष

Example: We reached the right conclusion.

Tip: Read each word aloud, then say its example sentence.
