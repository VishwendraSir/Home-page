# SCIENCE CENTRE LEARNING HUB — Class 6 Science Chapter 1

**The Wonderful World of Science** — Guided by Vishwendra Sir

## Included
- 20 bilingual topic lessons (Hindi / English / Both)
- Topic objectives, study notes, examples, key terms and activities
- Topic-wise MCQ checks and a 20-question chapter assessment
- Browser speech synthesis (no autoplay)
- YouTube URL embed player and local MP4/WebM picker
- Local student progress dashboard
- PWA manifest, icon and offline service-worker cache

## Important
- Progress is stored on this browser/device only; no login or cloud sync.
- YouTube needs internet. Local videos must be chosen from the device and are not bundled.
- Speech depends on browser/device voice availability.
- PWA installation and offline cache require HTTPS hosting.
- Login, payment, synced student accounts and downloadable certificates are not enabled in this release.
